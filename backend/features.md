# Features

## Side Quests Assignment
- Each user receives three side quests per week.
- Includes a reroll button to refresh the side quests, usable once per week.

## Weekly Cycle
- The week is fixed from Sunday to Saturday.

## Proof Submission
- Within the week, users can upload proof with a description for the completed task.

## Verification Process
- Once submitted for verification, the post is sent to 9 users.
- The first 5 users to validate it are used for voting.
- Voting with the majority increases your trust score; voting against reduces it.

## Rewards and Progression
- Successful completion increases your streak.
- Based on the toughness of the side quest, users gain XP and increases in different stats.
